# Privacy Policy — Etude

_Last updated: 2026-08-19_

Etude is a music practice app. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** Etude has no backend, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere.

## Data stored on your device

- Practice sessions, repertoire, streaks, and settings are stored in a local database on your device.
- Audio recordings of your practice are stored as files on your device only. The microphone is used solely to make these recordings, at your request, and recordings never leave your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Permissions

- **Microphone / background recording** — to record practice sessions you start, including while the screen is off.
- **Notifications** — to show recording and metronome controls on the lock screen.

## Children

Etude does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
